Features

Everything under one roof

Six powerful modules that cover every aspect of your transport and fleet operations.

📦

Operations

Manage the full order lifecycle — from booking to delivery. Generate and track Lorry Receipts with weight, checkpost, and charge details.

Orders Lorry Receipts (LR) e-Way Bill Checkposts
🚛

Fleet Management

Track every vehicle — RC, insurance, permits, PUC, GPS, fuel logs, and odometer readings. Schedule and record all service work.

Vehicle Profiles Fuel Logs Service Records Breakdowns
👥

HR Management

Manage drivers, cleaners, supervisors, and office staff. Track daily attendance, process monthly payroll, and handle salary advances.

Staff Profiles Attendance Payroll Salary Advances
🔧

Inventory

Maintain a spare parts catalog with live stock levels. Track all movements — purchases, service consumption, damage, and adjustments.

Spare Parts Stock Levels Stock Movements Low Stock Alerts

Tyre Management

Track every tyre across your fleet — position, fitting, rotation history, km per tyre, and replacement projections to reduce tyre costs.

Tyre Profiles Rotation Logs Km Tracking Replacement Forecast

Finance

Generate GST-ready invoices from LRs, record payments, issue credit notes, and track outstanding balances across all clients.

Invoices Credit Notes Payments Outstanding Tracking
