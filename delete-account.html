How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your FEROS account and associated data by following the steps below. Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.

⚠️ Account deletion is permanent. All your personal data, attendance records, and activity history will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered.
  1. Send an email to privacy@feros.in from your registered phone number's associated email, or ask your company's FEROS administrator to submit the request on your behalf.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request — [Your Name] — [Your Phone Number]"
  3. Include your full name, registered phone number, and company name in the email body.
  4. Our team will verify your identity and process the deletion within 7 business days.
  5. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted.

What Data is Deleted

  • Your personal profile (name, phone number, PIN)
  • Your attendance records and location check-in history
  • Your payroll and payslip data
  • Your authentication tokens and device sessions

What Data is Retained

The following data may be retained for legal and operational compliance:

  • Business transaction records (orders, LRs, invoices) that are part of your employer's legal records — retained as per Indian accounting regulations (typically 7 years)
  • Any data required by law to be retained

Note for Company Administrators

If you are a company administrator and wish to delete your entire tenant account and all associated data, please contact us directly. Upon subscription cancellation, all tenant data is automatically and permanently deleted after 90 days.
