Privacy Policy

Fleet Equipment Rental Operating System

FEROS ("we", "our", or "us") operates the FEROS mobile application (the "App") — a fleet management platform designed for transport companies. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our App.

By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1Information We Collect

a) Information you provide directly

  • Phone number and 4-digit PIN used to log in
  • Your name and role (Supervisor, Driver, Cleaner)
  • Photos uploaded for trip proof, vehicle images, and document scans
  • Odometer readings entered or captured via camera

b) Information collected automatically

  • Precise location — collected once when you mark attendance. Used solely to record the GPS coordinates of your attendance check-in. Not tracked continuously.
  • Device information — operating system version, device model, app version. Used for crash diagnostics and compatibility.

c) Business operation data

  • Orders, Lorry Receipts (LRs), vehicle assignments, fuel logs, meter readings
  • Attendance records and payroll information
  • All data entered in the course of your employment duties

2How We Use Your Information

Data Purpose
Phone number + PIN Authenticate your identity and provide access to the App
Precise location Record GPS coordinates for attendance check-in
Photos / camera Trip proof documentation, vehicle condition records, document uploads
Odometer photo (OCR) Extract meter reading automatically; processed on-device, not stored as image
Orders & LR data Core fleet operations — order management, lorry receipts, invoicing
Attendance & payroll Payroll calculation and HR management for your employer

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

3Data Storage and Security

  • Your authentication token (JWT) is stored in encrypted secure storage on your device using platform-level security (Android Keystore / iOS Secure Enclave).
  • All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS / TLS.
  • Business data is stored on our servers hosted on AWS (Mumbai region, ap-south-1) with regular automated backups.
  • Access to tenant data is strictly isolated — one company cannot access another company's data.
  • Authentication tokens expire after 24 hours and must be renewed by logging in again.
We do not store location data beyond the single GPS coordinate recorded at the time of attendance check-in. We do not track your location continuously or in the background.

4Permissions Required

Permission Reason When requested
Location (Precise) Record GPS at attendance check-in When marking attendance
Camera Capture trip proof photos, vehicle images, document scans When uploading photos
Photo library Select existing photos for upload When uploading photos
Internet Communicate with FEROS servers Always (core functionality)

All permissions are requested at runtime and can be revoked at any time through your device settings. Revoking a permission may limit certain features.

5Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party SDKs:

  • Google ML Kit (Text Recognition) — used for on-device OCR of odometer photos. No data is sent to Google; processing happens entirely on your device.
  • Google Fonts — Inter typeface loaded from Google's CDN at app startup.

We do not integrate with Facebook, analytics SDKs, or advertising networks.

6Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data may be shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Within your organisation — your employer (the transport company) has access to business data you enter (orders, attendance, LRs) as part of their subscription to FEROS.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or government authority.
  • Infrastructure providers — AWS for hosting, under strict data processing agreements.

7Data Retention

  • Business data (orders, LRs, attendance) is retained for the duration of your employer's active subscription.
  • Upon subscription cancellation, data is retained for 90 days and then permanently deleted.
  • Authentication tokens expire after 24 hours automatically.
  • Photos and documents are retained as long as the associated records exist.

8Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction — request corrections to inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal and operational requirements)
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact your company's FEROS administrator or reach us directly using the contact details below.

9Children's Privacy

The FEROS App is intended for use by employed adults aged 18 and above in a professional capacity. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that a person under 18 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
